Public Source · December 2025

The Pittsburgh Water Deliberation Record

Two years inside the board that runs Pittsburgh's water, coded vote by vote from the public minutes.

228votes coded
0dissents
$871.25Mauthorized
69%peer unanimity

223 of 228 resolutions passed unanimously while the board prepared to raise rates. At the first meeting after the story ran, the board asked about 40 questions, roughly seven times its two-year average, and issued a stop-work order.

Public Source · September 2025

The Pittsburgh Inclusionary Zoning Record

Six years of debate, and no agreed count of the affordable units the rule produced. So I built the count.

35units built
16under construction
100+proposed
3projects downsized

Three projects were shrunk to 19 units apiece, one below the 20-unit line that triggers the mandate. The record separates what is built from what is merely claimed.

West Hills Gazette · October 2025

The Pittsburgh Airport Terminal Record

A $1.75 billion terminal the Airport Authority would not fully cost out at its own ribbon-cutting. The audited record fills in the rest.

+54%cost, 2017 to 2025
$1.25Bbonds outstanding
$2.5Bdebt service to 2056
15M / 9.95Mbuilt for / used

The Authority named two grants and stopped counting. The 2024 audit shows $2.5 billion in debt service running to 2056, for a terminal built for up to 15 million passengers against 2024 demand under 10 million.
